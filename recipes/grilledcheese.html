Description

This recipe bring you back to good health!

Ingredients

  • 2 slices sandwich bread
  • 2 tablespoons softened butter
  • 2 slices cheddar cheese
  • 1 slice American or mozzarella cheese
  • Pinch of salt (optional)

Directions

  1. Spread butter evenly on one side of each slice of bread.
  2. Heat a skillet over medium-low heat.
  3. Place one slice of bread in the skillet, butter side down.
  4. Layer the cheese on top, then cover with the second slice of bread, butter side up.
  5. Cook for 3 to 4 minutes, until the bottom is deep golden brown.
  6. Carefully flip the sandwich and cook for another 3 minutes, until the cheese is melted and the second side is golden.
  7. Let it rest for 1 minute, then slice in half and serve warm.
