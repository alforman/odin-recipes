Description

This recipe brings Italy to your kitchen, and a smile to your face.

Ingredients

  • 10 tomatos
  • heavy cream
  • basil
  • rictotta
  • flour
  • eggs

Directions

  1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Simmer the tomatoes with a pinch of salt for 20 minutes, then stir in the basil to make the sauce.
  3. Melt a little butter in a pan, whisk in the flour, then slowly add the heavy cream and stir until thick and smooth.
  4. Mix the ricotta with a pinch of salt and pepper.
  5. Spread a thin layer of tomato sauce on the bottom of a baking dish.
  6. Add a layer of lasagna noodles, then spoon over some ricotta, cream sauce, and tomato sauce.
  7. Repeat the layers until you run out of ingredients, finishing with sauce on top.
  8. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes, then remove the foil and bake 15 minutes more, until golden and bubbling.
  9. Let the lasagna rest for 10 minutes before slicing and serving.
