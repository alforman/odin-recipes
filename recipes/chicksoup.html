Description

This recipe bring you back to good health!

Ingredients

  • 1 lb boneless chicken breasts or thighs
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 3 carrots, sliced
  • 3 celery stalks, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 8 cups chicken broth
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  • 2 cups egg noodles
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • Fresh parsley, chopped

Directions

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat.
  2. Add the onion, carrots, and celery. Cook for 5 minutes, stirring often, until they start to soften.
  3. Stir in the garlic and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  4. Pour in the chicken broth, then add the chicken, bay leaves, and thyme.
  5. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer for 20 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through.
  6. Remove the chicken, shred it with two forks, and set it aside.
  7. Add the egg noodles to the pot and cook for 6 to 8 minutes, until tender.
  8. Return the shredded chicken to the pot and remove the bay leaves.
  9. Season with salt and pepper, top with parsley, and serve hot.
